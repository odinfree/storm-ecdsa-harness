# Shared structural lane contract

This is the common rule for every quantum lane. Read the active project's contract first; do not alter its owner, queue, workers, clock or budget when maintaining skills.

## Start small
Use the [five-line first post](lane-card.md). Name the mechanism, source attach point and owned files, complete-path gain estimate, decisive falsifier and stop limit. Fill the integration card only at `integrated_candidate`.

Set the coordinator's measurable size threshold before work. The inspected ECDSA structural brief used 10% T at fixed Q, 10 Q at fixed T, or a whole stage removed; the feedback adds economically useful one-qubit cliffs. Record the active choice instead of carrying these thresholds into another campaign. Post kill arithmetic within two hours; stop at the named test/time/resource limit, on a correctness failure, or when the attainable gain falls below the threshold. Record the binding obstruction and a changed-premise reopen condition. A clean negative is a completed result.

Mine [existing records](../../quantum-record-mining/SKILL.md) before proposing a paper transfer. Reconcile ownership and old negatives before reopening a route.

## Admit a mechanism
Freeze parent source, cumulative patch, effective flags/dispatcher, toolchain, harness, supported inputs and source/emission hashes. Read the complete relevant paper construction, proof, appendices and limitations; state unread coverage. Map each premise to an actual source function, operation or lifetime. Price a missing primitive before building its scheduler. A paper title, helper name or toy success cannot establish transfer.

Account for all phases tied at peak Q and the next unaffected floor. Count both directions, both divisions, correction, reconstruction and representation boundaries. Use the current [front ceiling and cliff equations](benchmark-facts.md), not an old same-width row. Keep projected, emitted, executed and official counts distinct.

## Gate order
1. **Price probe.** This can kill bad economics; it cannot establish correctness.
2. **Native reachable-state test, before full emission.** Exercise ordinary inputs, stalled/off-model shots and known regressions through the real traversal. Check forward values, phase and scratch against independent specifications. An "exhaustive", "constant" or "provably zero" claim needs the actual reachable-state invariant; declarations and planner bounds are not a domain proof. A finite traversal pass is still not an all-input proof. Require a negative control at each rewrite site and state what observable result must fail.
3. **Integrated candidate.** Freeze the implementation and draw fresh confirmation inputs. Then run the promotion gate below; full emissions are expensive.

For borrowing, verify identity on arbitrary lender state, including a spectator. For measurements, preserve the channel, feed-forward and live classical outputs. Couple random outcomes by logical measurement role when possible; equal sequential RNG offsets after inserting measurements are not equivalent pairing. `U` followed by its own inverse does not prove its forward specification.

## ECDSA promotion gate — all five required
The build/panel details below come from the dated ECDSA shared brief and agreed promotion protocol, not from the papers. Explicit current project rules supersede that snapshot.
1. Two independent source-default builds with an empty process environment, pinned configuration/toolchain and identical emitted bytes/hash. Reading one file twice is not two builds. An off switch must reproduce the declared parent stream.
2. Fasteval unsalted plus 16 salts, with per-channel counts, lambda definition, uncertainty and the coordinator's pass criterion.
3. At least 16 **same-input paired corpora/runs** against the parent, preserving both failure identity sets and deltas. This does not mean 16 individual shots. Independent lambda panels cannot certify a lever. Any candidate-only failure means **not exact until explained**; a parent-shared coin-dependent failure needs a demonstrated cause, not similar aggregate counts.
4. A non-builder review with primary source/patch, build logs, test inputs/results and evaluator source. A proposed falsifier must identify a possible failing result; a check already forced on every passing shot adds no evidence.
5. A fresh official frontier read at promotion, bound to candidate Q, comparator row and timestamp. A new low-width row may dominate queued higher-width rows.

Exactness is relative to the declared parent semantics; an approximate parent is not all-input exact. Bind an exception to its authorized change, artifact and allowance; it never waives a gate. Missing receipts remain pending.

States: `hypothesis -> local_mechanism -> integrated_candidate -> independently_validated -> officially_evaluated`. Record scalar acceptance and Pareto membership separately. A scoped negative becomes `killed` or `deferred`; neither proves universal impossibility.
