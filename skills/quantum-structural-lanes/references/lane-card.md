# Five-line first post

1. Mechanism: what information, stage or binding allocation changes.
2. Attach point: source function, owner and non-overlapping files.
3. Gain: full-path Q/T arithmetic against the fresh ceiling; measured or projected.
4. Kill test: observable failure, size threshold and time/resource stop; arithmetic within two hours.
5. Source: parent/hash, paper sections or prior record; missing premises and prior negative.

No full card is required to investigate or close a negative.

# Integration card — only at integrated_candidate

- Identity: owner/files, parent and candidate source/patch/ops hashes, toolchain, harness, configuration, authorization/deadline/reservation.
- Contract: domain, objective, exactness or artifact-bound exception; paper coverage and premise-to-source map.
- Mechanism: before/after state, history, cleanup and interface; peak owners and remaining floor.
- Economics: projected and measured whole-path Q/T, current front comparator, conversion/correction overhead.
- Native evidence: reachable states, independent specification, per-site negative controls and retained failures.
- Promotion receipts: two builds, unsalted/16-salt panel, at least 16 same-input paired corpora with failure sets, non-builder primary-artifact review, fresh front.
- Delivery: official row/result and separate scalar/Pareto status.
- Decision: achieved, killed or deferred; missing gates, stop reason and concrete reopen condition.
