# 📥 Research Queue

Topics the engine works through, top-down. Items are checked off (`- [x]`) once
researched; the engine appends auto-generated follow-ups to the bottom.

**Item format:** `- [ ] [d=DEPTH] Question text (parent:: [[Parent Note]])`; top-level
items use `(parent:: seed)`. Researched items are checked; skipped ones (too deep, or already covered) are marked with a dash.
