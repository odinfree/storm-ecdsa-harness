# Source and transfer

[Discovering state-of-the-art quantum circuits with large language models v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22367v1), supplementary section II.1 and Appendix A.
The prime-field construction recovers branch decisions from successor registers when cheap and stores decisions otherwise. This exchanges history space for reconstruction work. The comparison covers complete point-add arithmetic but excludes application-specific addend lookup, so its totals are not automatically comparable to a windowed circuit.

Application hypothesis: successor-state predicates could replace stored history in a matching recurrence or inspire a separate architecture. A decoder theorem must retain its own signed-counter and arithmetic premises.
