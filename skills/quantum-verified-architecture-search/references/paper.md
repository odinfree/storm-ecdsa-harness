# Sources and transfer

[Agentic compilation v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.23832v2), section II: restricted reversible/phase/measurement structure enables efficient simulation and comparison with classical references while tracking phase. Fuzzing supports distribution-specific error evidence under stated assumptions, not universal correctness.

[LLM circuit discovery v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22367v1), main framework: models propose designs while an independent evaluator verifies and scores them. Its modular-inversion mechanism has a separate `quantum-divstep-reconstruction` lane.

The skill's artifact separation, adversarial evaluator fixtures and board promotion procedure are engineering applications of this approach; successful skill execution does not imply a new record.
