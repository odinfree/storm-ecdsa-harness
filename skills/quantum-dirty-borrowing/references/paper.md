# Source and transfer

[Bona v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.08765v1), sections 3–6, especially Algorithm 1.
Bona models safe borrowing through dependency-graph changes. Its scheduler first seeks depth-neutral placements and otherwise searches feasible splices with a depth-aware heuristic. A topology-changing splice requires updated graph information. The method is heuristic, not an optimality certificate; its reported dirty-ancilla reduction is not a comparable percentage reduction in total circuit width.

Application hypothesis: a peak-bound emitter may benefit from sharing physical storage across independently valid borrowing intervals. The skill's board experiment and promotion rules are proposed engineering work, not a result demonstrated for our source by the paper.
