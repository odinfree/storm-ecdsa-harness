# Source and transfer

[Measurement-Based Uncomputation from an Error Correction Perspective v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.31605v1), sections II, IV.1–IV.4 and Appendix F.
Measurement-induced phase patterns can be grouped into syndrome classes. Under the paper's orthogonal-branch assumptions, accepting a set A of rank-r classes gives success probability |A|/2^r; nonorthogonal branches require different analysis. Expected repetitions additionally assume independent attempts and input re-preparation. The oracle-reuse correction construction has structural premises and is not universal.

Application hypothesis: a small correction basis could lower complete cleanup cost. No claim is made that a benchmark failure penalty accepts post-selection.
