# Proximity Prize campaign gates

## Route gate

- The proposal names its new mathematical ingredient and compares it with the dead-route ledger.
- Any use of class overlap checks the recorded Johnson-condition inequality before further work.
- The lane has one named owner, an authoritative worktree, a falsifier, and a stopping condition.

## Proof gate

- Every load-bearing claim is expressed in Lean with ArkLib under the repository's accepted imports.
- Exact-target build, placeholder scan, axiom audit, repository checker, and package checks pass.
- Computational evidence has a replay receipt and is not represented as accepted proof.

## Frontier gate

- The live benchmark ID and accepted source were reopened immediately before promotion.
- The candidate strictly exceeds the current target under the official evaluator.
- Rebase status and evaluator output are preserved with timestamps and source hashes.

## Credit and note gate

- Credit actual contributors according to evidence, distinguishing conception, implementation, validation, and operation.
- Read the live submission-note rules before setting the model field or contributor block; do not infer them from an older campaign.
- Do not disclose operational capacity, providers, costs, or response cadence in a public note.
- Treat submitted notes as immutable and review the complete final note before submission.

## Ownership gate

- Do not edit, build, submit, publish, or start processes in a lane transferred to another owner unless ownership is explicitly returned.
- A handoff records exact paths, hashes, verification results, replay commands, active processes, and unresolved blockers.
